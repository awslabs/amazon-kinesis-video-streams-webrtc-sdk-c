◆ DTLS_IN_STUN_MAX_STASHED_PACKETS

#define DTLS_IN_STUN_MAX_STASHED_PACKETS   4

SPED (draft-uberti-tsvwg-warp, section 4.2) carries the DTLS handshake inside ICE connectivity checks: each Binding Request and Response embeds one DTLS packet of the current flight, round-robin, and acknowledges the embedded or plain DTLS packets received so far by their CRC-32. The controller below holds that state for one ICE agent; the ICE agent and the DTLS session feed it. It takes its own lock and never calls out.
